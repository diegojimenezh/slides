 \end{itemize}}

     \item [\textcolor{accent}{$\rightarrow$}] Arrows, boxes and labels to use anywhere in a slide and a way to place them easily

\begin{frame}[t]{How to Use the Template}

\vspace{2em}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Edit the metadata at the top of \texttt{slides.tex}: title, authors, date, presenter. \vfill
\item Swap the numbered files in \texttt{sections/} for your own, in the order you present them. \vfill
\item Change the look from the \texttt{USER SETTINGS} block in \texttt{preamble.tex}. \vfill
\item Compile with \texttt{latexmk slides.tex}; intermediate files go to \texttt{.textmp/}.
\end{enumerate}

\vfill
